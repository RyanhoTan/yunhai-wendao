# Bark Brown 01 · CC0

Original asset: [Poly Haven Bark Brown 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/bark_brown_01), by Rob Tuytel.

Poly Haven publishes these textures under [CC0 1.0](https://polyhaven.com/license). The original 1K JPG diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness files are unchanged. API metadata, original download URLs, sizes, MD5 and SHA-256 are stored beside them.

The texture is physically 1 metre wide; the game's axial/cross-section metre UVs are mapped with the grain along the wood. This is local deadwood surface detail, not terrain geometry or a lighting replacement.
