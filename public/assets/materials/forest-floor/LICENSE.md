# Forest Floor — Poly Haven

Original 1K diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness maps by **eye-candy.xyz**.

- Asset: https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_floor
- License: CC0 1.0, https://polyhaven.com/license
- License text: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Physical scan width: 2.1 metres.

Files are the original downloaded maps. API metadata, byte sizes, MD5 and
SHA-256 values are retained in `api-files.json` and `source.json`.

The material supplies soil and decomposing litter beneath real scene plants.
It does not replace the game's physical grass, ferns, leaves or shrub geometry.
