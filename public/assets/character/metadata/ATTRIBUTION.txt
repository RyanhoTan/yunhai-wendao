Adapted for Yunhai Wendao: FBX-to-GLB conversion, duplicate-vertex welding,
skin-weight normalization, retained terminal joints, PBR materials,
1024px WebP textures and FBX/glTF UV-direction correction. The game's authored
face, hair and costume changes replace the source character's samurai identity.
No endorsement by the original creator is implied.

Animations: Adobe Mixamo, via SamuraiThirdPersonTemplateThreeJS.
Game use is permitted by Adobe's Mixamo FAQ:
https://helpx.adobe.com/creative-cloud/faq/mixamo-faq.html
Only horizontal Hips translation is made in-place; vertical motion is preserved.
